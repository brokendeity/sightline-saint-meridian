## Open work / failures (required before release)

The visual result is an early structural prototype and does NOT closely match the supplied references yet. One bridge and several gallery/terrace/support placements are rejected by real building clearance; rejection records remain visible. Replace whole-span rejection with supported segmented assembly and doorway/landing connections. Stair risers exist, but full route connectivity, camera clearance and normal-control ground→upper→street traversal have NOT passed acceptance. Dense collider count needs spatial batching and profiling. Add the elevated vehicle road, real navigable destination graph, facade modules, warm interior/soffit lighting and pedestrian destinations. Current vehicle types share a placeholder body; no distinct production models, traffic merges, near-field collision/avoidance, vehicle physics, audio or train passenger interaction. Train turning radius/car envelopes and complete viaduct support continuity need refinement; current static sample clearance is insufficient certification of swept multi-car clearance.

Further gates: original missions/destruction/traversal regressions; actual cartridge anchors/surf/Drive/rail Pop across added structures; every collision proxy tested for valid support; high/medium/low tiers without gameplay changes; 60s matched baseline performance with CPU p50/p95/triangles/memory/load time and traversal; reference A/B and additional gameplay views iterated to fidelity; editable transit assets if modeled; full gameplay video. No original-reference similarity or performance target is claimed passed. No GitHub push or final accepted release yet.
