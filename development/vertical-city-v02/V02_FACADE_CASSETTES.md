## Architecture and source authority

The 2,428 closed annular surrounds are source-supported attachments to original building owners 141 (1,117), 160 (837) and 162 (474). They are not substitute tower shells or doorways. Each surrounds an existing window without covering its 1.68 × 2.36 m glazing/frame opening. The outer chamfered surround is 3.10 × 3.80 m; its inner aperture is 1.90 × 2.56 m. All new visible geometry lies inside a 7.8 cm source envelope. The old window, trim and tower geometry remain unchanged.

The admission planner subtracts original projected source triangles and compound rectangles from every annular quad. The whole supported footprint is checked, rather than the centre only. Original projecting fittings and current V02 solids exclude conflicting candidates. The author opens the upper master, creates 2,428 individually identified source components and three merged export nodes, and saves/reopens a new master. The independent validator checks previous objects, local/world transforms, parents, material nodes and bindings, packed images and lights. It checks the new closed positive-volume manifolds and original window openings.

All 155,392 shipping triangles are independently matched, with cyclic orientation, to reopened Blender corner witnesses. Position and UV errors are zero; maximum normal-component difference is 0.00006253148. Khronos reports zero errors and warnings on all three assets. Nine shared material draw groups are used across the three owners. Runtime gzip totals approximately 2.9 MB; this is distinct from decoded/raw geometry memory.

## Runtime and ownership

The child entry appends one asset adapter to the frozen parent. It waits for current diffuse assets, verifies compressed and decoded hashes, identifies the original source owner, and commits all three meshes atomically. The attachments follow their actual original mesh transforms and original near/far visibility. No new dynamic lights, colliders, walking surfaces, input rules or power changes are introduced.

Damage cleanup invalidates unsupported source-backed surrounds, collapsing their actual geometry after an original owner breach; complete owner collapse hides the attachment root. The native test proves all 310 original geometry attributes, indices, groups, material identities, matrices and compound boxes remain identical before/after loading. Its breach/collapse setup is explicitly a direct native API fixture, **not legal-input destruction acceptance**.

It includes the full lower, middle and high public loops; sampled room travel/boot visibility/combat framing at desktop, portrait and landscape layouts; the installed web-game skill client; six zero/full-bank station approaches; a complete short-catch mastery recovery; and 12 current service/combat/replay/market/pursuit/discovery cases. It records world draw calls/triangles, asynchronous GPU queries, simulation CPU, render submission CPU, frame intervals, heap endpoints, load time, exact actor/camera traces, video and runtime errors. The terminal measurements and their limitations are in `V02_Facade_Cassettes_Benchmark.json`; do not substitute a post-processing render count or a single screenshot for world cost.
