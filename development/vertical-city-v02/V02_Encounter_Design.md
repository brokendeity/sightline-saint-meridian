## Transit interchange pursuit

Station concourse: X58–75, Z359–393, deck Y100. Keep the concourse west of the moving guideway; a pedestrian destination is not permission to intersect a train envelope. Public stair C enters from the Y84 west gallery. Upper stair D reaches the north access deck. The two-part momentum approach arrives from a supported Y92 landing; the upper Pop approach arrives from above. Distinct elevations and approach directions must remain visible from the concourse.

Use a bounded pursuit runner with a deterministic supported route, trigger through an in-world interaction, and allow interception by the existing player/combatant distance rules. A new pursuit encounter adapter is required; scenic traffic is not a pursuit actor. The runner must stay on this route, slow for corners, pause with the game and retain its state during hero switching. The player can intercept at the entrance, cut across the gallery, or follow from above. The destination is a clear 8×8 m exit region, not an arbitrary timer.

Acceptance: start/finish/retry through real interaction input; both chase and interception; missed upper landing recovery; no runner/train overlap; route state survives pause and unrelated hero objectives.

Implemented candidate prototype: `scripts/game_vertical_pursuit_v02.js`. Access[73,100,364], runner[73,100,373]. The route stays on the real east side of the station stairwell opening, reaches stairD at[61,100,403.5], climbs the physical switchback to128m and continues via the west gallery and upper crossing to[84,128,420]. The safe8×8m interception/landing area X76–84/Z416–424 lies on the observation deck; the runner's exit point sits on its eastern boundary. This route is separate from the train guideway. Full carriage sweep clearance remains a separate pending gate.

At access, press **I**, controller **Y interaction** or the context button. Follow the outer route, or reach the observation deck early and intercept across it. Interception requires grounded player proximity within2.25m, compatible height and an unobstructed ray. There is no invisible success timer, contact damage or imitation melee system. One400point award is granted per explicit attempt. Reaching the actual upper exit produces escape rather than failure by an arbitrary timeout. Retry at the access marker preserves player condition. Explicit restart may reset the runner to its fixed start; active movement never teleports it.

The runner uses the existing scenic pedestrian prototype appearance and native actor-ground/body clearance, with a dedicated route controller. Its2.7m/s horizontal movement budget, <=.2m support transitions, corner pauses and collision checks apply each simulation frame. Original actor sweeps snagged stair risers on actual C/D ascent and descent. The V02-only adapter excludes the capsule's leading/trailing contact with explicitly tagged new low risers, while native centre support limits and all other walls, headroom and ledges stay authoritative. Four controlled stair probes and solid-wall/counter/unsupported-edge rejection cases test that distinction.

Pause freezes route progress and clocks. Hero switching pauses the same attempt without replacing its runner; Backspool resumes it through the normal interaction binding. End preserves the last supported runner position. Respawn/defeat produce an interrupted outcome. Conflicting street contracts and other local encounters cannot replace an active pursuit. Missing route support or obstruction is reported, not resolved by warping or altering player powers.

Current chase/interception, escape and lifecycle evidence is in `V02_Pursuit_*.json`, complete compressed traces and actual captures. The local tests have one labelled station setup; a separate full ground-to-pursuit approach is being validated. Capture screenshots exposed an inherited hidden-actor flag; the runner clone is now explicitly visible and the stopped sole/neutral pose plus camera framing are checked. Prototype art, wider camera/recovery/stealth/combat interactions and full moving-transit clearance remain unaccepted. Do not promote the whole V02 quality gate from these cases.

## Suspended-market civilian rescue

Main deck: X58–93, Z353–379, Y24 (35×26 m). Two gallery entrances and the lower return crossing serve this public destination. Reserve a 6 m through-route; merchant stalls sit off it. Two civilians circulate on supported loops and through marked entrances. An incident occurs beside the central stall while the extraction point stays on the same deck, reachable without rails, Drive or courier flight.

The original courier rescue supports rooftop pickup/carry, not a civilian escort. The new candidate therefore owns a separate incident adapter in `scripts/game_vertical_market_v02.js`; it reuses current scenic pedestrian appearance, shared hero health and native ground/body clearance. It never registers civilians as Halo combatants or modifies courier passengers, roof civilians or rescue anchors.

Implemented graybox: two physical counters at[71,24,365] and[83,24,369], with visible posts and canopies at27.7m. Their underside is3.4m above the market floor. A real isolation cabinet stands at[82,24,355.4], with interaction stance[82,24,357]. Resident[85,24,364], incident access[62,24,375], assembly[62,24,357]. Two other residents circulate on clearance-checked paths. Both public gallery entrances remain clear. A clear8×8m landing region is available atX58.5–66.5/Z362–370.5.

Press **I**, controller **Y interaction**, or the contextual button. Find the resident outside the amber fault ring, isolate the supply, return to begin the escort, then walk to green assembly. The live fault applies8shared-health damage per1.2simulation seconds on actual contact; isolation stops it. It can cause a real defeat. The escort follows a bounded queue of the player's physically supported footsteps at up to2.2m/s. Jumps, blocked shortcuts and leaving the market produce a waiting explanation and a route-reconnection cue. No catch-up teleport is used. Explicit Start/Retry resets the resident to the fixed incident position; End leaves the resident at their last safe position.

Pause freezes the local clock and all residents. Hero switching pauses rescue progress and the escort route; ambient residents still use the active world clock. Returning as Backspool resumes the same attempt. Defeat/respawn retain a failed outcome; Start never restores condition. Successful extraction requires both resident and player in the assembly area and awards450once per attempt. Completed objectives relinquish HUD ownership when the player leaves the market. Existing street contracts cannot be replaced by beginning the rescue, and cannot start over an active rescue.

`V02_Market_Rescue_Public.json` demonstrates the entire ordinary ground→stairsA→lower gallery→market approach and the complete incident through normal inputs. `V02_Market_Rescue_South.json` demonstrates the longer southern flank from one labelled local setup. The starts differ, so their total times are not a speed comparison. Both routes preserve the original courier mission snapshot, keep actor bases atY24 and bound per-frame movement. `V02_Market_Lifecycle.json` covers pause, hero switching, native jumping, leaving/reconnecting, End, controller/CDP-touch starts, real fault defeat and labelled respawn/negative API cases. Current hashes and the aggregate report are the acceptance authority.
