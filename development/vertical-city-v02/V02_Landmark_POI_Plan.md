# V02 landmarks and discovery — design and admission gates

Three landmarks must be recognizable during ordinary gameplay, at speed and from both reference bookmarks. Graybox massing is followed by architectural refinement only after the connected loops pass.

- **Elevated transit interchange**, [66.5,100,376]: a long concourse west of the train guideway, ribbed canopy, warm station interior and thin cyan platform edges. Public entrances, departure information and maintenance access establish its function. Preserve the real Hi3D front carriage; middle/rear consistency and full swept train validation remain open.
- **Suspended commercial complex**, [75.5,24,366]: a wide inhabited market with shaded amber shop fronts, public routes along its sides, clear circulation and visible upper service access. Its broad horizontal silhouette contrasts with the station. No shops terminate at blank imported facades without a genuine portal.
- **Upper observation structure**, [72,128,420]: an open viewing terrace with an offset modern canopy and narrow beacon. Public stair B and the upper crossing make this a destination. An upper rail line rewards traversal mastery, with its landing and recovery constraints tested separately.

Four meaningful discoverable locations:

1. **Market incident point**, [64,24,366]: civilian rescue start and safe extraction on the same deck.
2. **Maintenance access**, [86,52,320]: an alternate entry into the service yard, with support equipment and a flank route.
3. **Observation deck**, [72,128,420]: a readable city overlook, district route orientation and a return to public circulation.
4. **Station signal desk**, [68,100,382]: transit information and the pursuit encounter's destination/context.

Discovery is proximity plus matching supported elevation, not proximity in an XZ map. A one-time discovery record may mark the location; it must not repeatedly award points. Every POI needs a clear public approach, physical support, a useful interaction or vantage, and a tested way out. Names are functional labels, not invented canon history.

The approved palette remains midnight-blue/charcoal, restrained cyan trim, amber recessed light and tinted glass. Reference towers remain imported authored assets. Do not obscure weak geometry with bloom or use graybox cubes as final architecture.
